下面这 15 句话，都是母语者在日常生活、工作、聊天中反复出现的高频表达。不是花哨表达，但非常地道。

背熟例句，自然使用！

1️⃣ It really comes down to…
说到底还是……

It really comes down to how much time you have.
说到底还是看你有多少时间。

At the end of the day, it really comes down to priorities.
归根结底还是优先级的问题。

———

2️⃣ I get what you’re saying.
我懂你的意思。

I get what you’re saying, but I see it a bit differently.
我懂你的意思，不过我看法不太一样。

Yeah, I get what you’re saying now.
嗯，我现在明白你的意思了。

———

3️⃣ That’s fair.
这也有道理。

That’s fair. I didn’t think about it that way.
这也有道理，我之前没这么想过。

Yeah, that’s fair given the situation.
嗯，在这种情况下这么说也合理。

———

4️⃣ I’d say…
我觉得……

I’d say this is the best option for now.
我会说这是目前最好的选择。

I’d say he’s doing a pretty good job.
我觉得他做得挺不错的。

———

5️⃣ From my experience…
以我的经验来看……

From my experience, consistency matters more than talent.
以我的经验来看，坚持比天赋更重要。

From my experience, people overthink this.
根据我的经验，很多人把这件事想复杂了。

———

6️⃣ It doesn’t really matter.
其实没那么重要 / 无所谓。

It doesn’t really matter where we eat.
在哪吃其实都无所谓。

In the long run, it doesn’t really matter.
从长远来看，这并不重要。

———

7️⃣ I’m fine either way.
怎么都行（两种选项）。

I’m fine either way — you can decide.
我怎么都行，你来决定吧。

Morning or afternoon works. I’m fine either way.
早上或下午都可以，我都行。

———

8️⃣ That works for me.
我这边可以。

Friday afternoon? Yeah, that works for me.
周五下午？可以的。

If that works for you, it works for me too.
如果你那边可以，我也没问题。

———

9️⃣ I see your point.
我明白你的观点。

I see your point, but I still disagree.
我明白你的观点，但我还是不同意。

I see your point about the cost.
我理解你对费用的担忧。

———

🔟 It’s not a big deal.
没什么大不了的。

Don’t worry about it. It’s not a big deal.
别担心，这不是什么大事。

Being late once isn’t a big deal.
偶尔迟到一次没什么。

———

1️⃣1️⃣ I’ll take care of it.
我来处理。

Don’t worry, I’ll take care of it.
别担心，我来处理。

You focus on that — I’ll take care of the rest.
你专心做那个，其他的我来搞定。

———

1️⃣2️⃣ I’ll let you know.
我到时候告诉你。

I’m not sure yet, but I’ll let you know.
我现在还不确定，之后告诉你。

I’ll let you know once I hear back.
一有消息我就告诉你。

———

1️⃣3️⃣ That’s how I see it.
我是这么看的。

That’s how I see it, based on what we know.
根据目前的信息，我是这么看的。

You might disagree, but that’s how I see it.
你可能不同意，但这是我的看法。

———

1️⃣4️⃣ At the end of the day…
归根结底……

At the end of the day, health comes first.
归根结底，健康最重要。

At the end of the day, it’s your decision.
说到底，还是你自己决定。

———

1️⃣5️⃣ It is what it is.
也只能这样了/就是这么个情况。

We did our best. It is what it is.
我们已经尽力了，也只能这样了。

It’s frustrating, but it is what it is.
很让人无奈，但现实就是这样。
